# Initial support matrix

| Family | Releases | Architectures | Init | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rocky Linux | 8, 9 | amd64, arm64* | systemd | planned certification |
| AlmaLinux | 8, 9 | amd64, arm64* | systemd | planned certification |
| Debian | 12 | amd64, arm64* | systemd | planned certification |
| Ubuntu | 22.04, 24.04 | amd64, arm64* | systemd | planned certification |

`*` A product/OS/version combination is supported only when its vendor publishes
a matching artifact and the complete VM test matrix passes.

CentOS 7/8, openEuler, Anolis OS, Kylin, and UOS may be detected but are not
claimed as supported in the first release.
